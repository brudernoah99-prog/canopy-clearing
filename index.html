Canopy Clearing: Facility 7

You have no legs. Push your hands against the floor, walls and crates to move. Swing your arms and let go to jump.

Rip an arm off: grab your other arm with GRIP and yank it away. Throw it, or grab it off the floor again.

Regrow it: step into the green REGEN POD. Test dummies: punch them apart. Red button: become the monster.
